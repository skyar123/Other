# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & SPECIFICATIONS

## Step 4.1: Create Roof Frame
**Instructions:**
1. Start at FRONT of house (deck side)
2. At level 10 (top of second floor walls), begin roof
3. Front edge: Start at height 10
4. Back edge: End at height 6 (4 block drop over 14 block depth)

**Roof Slope Pattern (using DARK OAK STAIRS):**

**Row 1 (Front edge, level 10):**
- Place 11 DARK OAK STAIRS facing south (upside down)
- Stairs run the full width of house

**Row 2 (level 10):**
- Move 1 block back
- Place 11 DARK OAK STAIRS facing south

**Row 3-14 (Continue pattern):**
- Every 3 blocks back, drop height by 1
- Block 1-3: height 10
- Block 4-6: height 9
- Block 7-9: height 8
- Block 10-12: height 7
- Block 13-14: height 6

## Step 5.3: Install Wire Mesh Railing (Signature Feature)
Use IRON BARS placed HORIZONTALLY to create wire mesh effect

**Instructions:**
1. Around 3 edges of deck (front, left, right - NOT house side)
2. Place IRON BARS at 2 heights:
   - First rail: 1 block above deck surface
   - Second rail: 2 blocks above deck surface
3. Iron bars will connect automatically, creating horizontal mesh

```
Front View of Railing:

    [DECK POST]
         │
         ├─[IRON BAR]─┬─[IRON BAR]─┐  ← Top rail
         ├─[IRON BAR]─┬─[IRON BAR]─┐  ← Bottom rail
    ─────┴────────────┴─────────────  ← Deck surface
         │            │
    [POST]          [POST]

Side View:
     POST
      │
      ├══════════  ← Iron bar rail (top)
      ├══════════  ← Iron bar rail (bottom)
    ──┴──────────  ← Deck floor
```

**Railing Pattern:**
1. Place iron bars between support posts
2. At corners, bars will connect at right angles
3. Create modern, open wire mesh appearance
4. DO NOT use solid blocks - keep it transparent

# APPENDIX A: TROUBLESHOOTING

## Problem: Concrete won't convert from powder
**Solution:**
- Concrete powder must touch water source or flowing water
- Place powder next to water block
- Use bucket to pour water over powder
- Powder will instantly convert to solid concrete

## Problem: Iron bars connecting vertically instead of horizontally
**Solution:**
- Iron bars auto-connect to adjacent blocks
- For horizontal rails, ensure no blocks above/below
- Place bars in continuous line along railing path
- They will connect horizontally between posts

## Problem: Stairs placing upside-down
**Solution:**
- Stair orientation depends on where you click
- Click bottom half of block = stairs right-side up
- Click top half = stairs upside down (for roof underside)
- For roof: place while looking up at underside

## Problem: Roof has gaps or looks uneven
**Solution:**
- Use only stairs for roof (not slabs or planks)
- Ensure stairs all face same direction (south for front-to-back slope)
- Place in consistent pattern row by row
- Check for missing stair blocks

## Problem: Rooms feel dark even with lighting
**Solution:**
- Add more torches or lanterns
- Place light sources at floor level and ceiling
- Use glowstone or sea lanterns for brighter ambient light
- Check for blocked light paths

## Problem: Deck railing looks wrong or incomplete
**Solution:**
- Iron bars must be placed on 3 sides only (not house side)
- Place two levels of bars (at heights 1 and 2 above deck)
- Bars will auto-connect between posts
- Ensure posts are at corners and intervals

# APPENDIX B: MATERIAL GATHERING GUIDE

## Where to Find Materials

### Gray Concrete (400 blocks needed)
**Ingredients:**
- **Gray Dye (400):** Combine black dye + white dye
  - Black dye: From ink sacs (kill squids) or wither roses
  - White dye: From bone meal (craft from bones)
- **Sand (1,600):** Desert biomes, beaches
- **Gravel (3,200):** Rivers, underwater, mountains

**Crafting Process:**
1. Craft gray dye: 1 black dye + 1 white dye = 2 gray dye
2. Craft concrete powder: 4 sand + 4 gravel + 1 gray dye = 8 gray concrete powder
3. Convert to concrete: Place powder next to water

### White Concrete (200 blocks needed)
**Ingredients:**
- **Bone Meal (200):** Craft from bones (skeleton drops)
- **Sand (800):** Desert/beaches
- **Gravel (1,600):** Rivers/mountains

**Crafting Process:**
1. Bone → 3 bone meal
2. 4 sand + 4 gravel + 1 bone meal = 8 white concrete powder
3. Place powder next to water to convert

### Birch Planks (100 blocks needed)
**Source:** Birch trees (white bark)
- 1 birch log = 4 birch planks
- Need: 25 birch logs

### Spruce Planks (100 blocks needed)
**Source:** Spruce trees (dark bark, taiga biome)
- 1 spruce log = 4 spruce planks
- Need: 25 spruce logs

### Dark Oak Stairs (200 needed)
**Source:** Craft from dark oak planks
- 6 dark oak planks = 4 stairs
- Need: 300 dark oak planks = 75 dark oak logs
- **Dark oak logs:** Roofed forest biome (2×2 trees)

### Iron Bars (80 needed)
**Source:** Craft from iron ingots
- 6 iron ingots = 16 iron bars
- Need: 30 iron ingots
- **Iron ingots:** Smelt iron ore (found underground Y: 0-64)

### Glass (80 blocks needed)
**Source:** Smelt sand in furnace
- 1 sand = 1 glass block
- Need: 80 sand + fuel (coal/charcoal)

### Dark Oak Trapdoors (60 needed)
**Source:** Craft from dark oak planks
- 6 dark oak planks = 2 trapdoors
- Need: 180 dark oak planks = 45 dark oak logs

### Oak Planks (300 needed - flooring)
**Source:** Oak trees (common in most biomes)
- 1 oak log = 4 oak planks
- Need: 75 oak logs

### Smooth Stone (200 blocks needed)
**Source:** Smelt stone (not cobblestone) in furnace
- Cobblestone → (smelt) → Stone → (smelt) → Smooth Stone
- Need: 200 cobblestone + fuel for double smelting

## Efficient Gathering Order
1. **Wood gathering first:** Find and harvest:
   - 75 oak logs
   - 25 birch logs
   - 25 spruce logs
   - 120 dark oak logs
2. **Mining session:** Underground for:
   - 30 iron ore (for bars)
   - Coal for smelting fuel
   - Cobblestone for foundation
3. **Desert/beach trip:**
   - 2,480 sand (for concrete + glass)
4. **River/gravel gathering:**
   - 4,800 gravel (for concrete)
5. **Mob farming (night):**
   - Skeletons for bones → bone meal
   - Squids for ink sacs → black dye
