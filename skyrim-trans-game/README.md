# Transition Quest: 2025 America

A 2D open-world RPG inspired by Skyrim, focused on transgender experiences and activism in 2025 America.

## Overview

**Transition Quest** is a politically-charged role-playing game that explores the trans experience through the lens of adventure, community, and resistance. Set in 2025 America, players navigate a divided nation, meeting legendary trans icons and confronting real-world antagonists who oppose trans rights.

## Features

### Character Creation
- **Gender Identity Options**: Trans Woman, Trans Man, Non-Binary, Genderfluid, Agender, Questioning
- **Skill System**: Distribute points across 5 key attributes:
  - **Advocacy**: Ability to speak truth and influence others
  - **Resilience**: Capacity to withstand hate and bounce back
  - **Knowledge**: Understanding of trans history and rights
  - **Empathy**: Connection with others and emotional strength
  - **Courage**: Bravery to face opposition

### Open World Exploration
Explore a procedurally generated map of America with key locations including:
- **Safe Havens**: San Francisco Pride District, NYC Stonewall Plaza, Austin Queer Quarter, Seattle Capitol Hill, Portland Activist Hub
- **Hostile Territories**: Nashville Bible Belt Crossroads, Dallas Conservative Stronghold
- **Political Centers**: Washington DC Capitol
- **Neutral Zones**: Phoenix Desert Resistance

### Trans Icons as NPCs (Allies)
Meet and learn from legendary trans advocates:
- **Laverne Cox** - Teaches resilience and courage
- **Elliot Page** - Shares advocacy strategies
- **Janet Mock** - Imparts trans history knowledge
- **Sam Smith** - Expands understanding of non-binary identities
- **Chaz Bono** - Shares pioneering trans experience
- **Indya Moore** - Teaches intersectional struggle
- **Jazz Jennings** - Offers hope and optimism
- **Trans Elders** - Share decades of wisdom
- **Allied Organizers** - Teach effective allyship

### Enemies (Real-World Transphobes)
Confront actual political and media figures who oppose trans rights through debate battles:
- **Politicians**: Ron DeSantis, Marjorie Taylor Greene, Greg Abbott
- **Media Personalities**: Tucker Carlson, Matt Walsh, Ben Shapiro
- **Authors/Celebrities**: JK Rowling
- **Random Transphobes**: Representing everyday bigotry

### Quest System
Complete meaningful quests that reflect real trans experiences:
- **Main Quests**:
  - Find Your Community
  - Learn from the Icons
  - Stand Against Hate
- **Side Quests**:
  - Journey of Self-Discovery
  - Explore different cities across America

### Combat System
Engage in "debate battles" where you use:
- **Debate Attack**: Use facts and compassion
- **Special Ability**: Unleash knowledge and courage (costs energy)
- **Defend**: Steel yourself with resilience
- **Retreat**: Live to fight another day

### Progression & Resources
- **Level System**: Gain XP, level up, increase skills
- **Health**: Your physical wellbeing
- **Energy**: Powers special abilities
- **Hope**: Increases in safe havens, decreases from defeats
- **Inventory**: Collect meaningful items and trophies

## How to Play

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Move your character
- **Space**: Interact with NPCs, locations, and enemies
- **I**: View inventory (future feature)
- **Q**: View quest log (future feature)
- **ESC**: Menu (future feature)

### Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. Click "Begin Your Journey" on the title screen
3. Create your character by selecting:
   - Your name
   - Your gender identity
   - Your starting skill distribution (10 points total)
4. Explore the world, meet allies, and fight bigotry!

### Gameplay Tips
- Visit **Safe Haven** cities to restore Hope and Energy
- Talk to **Trans Icons** (heart symbol ♥) to gain skills and wisdom
- Engage **Enemies** (× symbol) in debate battles to progress quests
- Explore all 10 major cities to complete side quests
- Use your special ability strategically - it costs energy!

## Game Mechanics

### Character Stats
- **Health**: 100 (increases with level)
- **Energy**: 100 (increases with level, regenerates slowly)
- **Hope**: 50-100 (increases in safe spaces, decreases from defeats)

### Skills Impact
- **Advocacy**: Increases debate attack damage
- **Resilience**: Reduces enemy damage taken
- **Knowledge**: Powers special abilities
- **Empathy**: Improves dialogue options and NPC interactions
- **Courage**: Enhances special attack damage

### Win Conditions
There is no single "win" state - the game is about the journey:
- Complete all main quests
- Defeat all major transphobes
- Meet all trans icons
- Reach maximum level
- Explore all locations

## Political & Social Commentary

This game is explicitly political and takes a strong stance in support of:
- Trans rights are human rights
- Healthcare access for trans individuals
- Legal protections against discrimination
- Representation in media and politics
- The validity of all gender identities

The game portrays real public figures who have opposed trans rights as antagonists. This is intentional social commentary on the very real harm caused by anti-trans rhetoric and legislation in America.
